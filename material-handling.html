How we can help

We assess engine ECU programming, software recovery and fault enquiries against the equipment and controller fitted. Please identify the controller involved so we can confirm whether it falls within our services.

Specialist ECU cloning

Cloning and engine-management data transfer are core services. We assess the original ECU and compatible replacement before agreeing the work. Read about ECU cloning →

ECU work we can assess

  • Diagnostic support for ECU faults and communication problems.
  • Cloning, data transfer and replacement ECU programming where compatible.
  • Software recovery and corrupted-data support.
  • Remapping and calibration suited to the application and intended use.

Available services depend on the ECU and the fault. Contact us first to confirm suitability.

Tell us about the job

Equipment make and model, engine or power type, controller part number and a description of the fault.

Include photographs of the ECU label if possible. We will confirm whether mail-in work is suitable and whether additional modules or access to the vehicle, machine or engine are needed.
