Discuss your job

Fuel economy matters

Economy-focused calibration for cars and tractors, with attention to usable torque, drivability and the way the vehicle or machine works. Potential savings depend on the engine, its condition, driving style, load and operating conditions; a particular fuel saving is not guaranteed.

Cars and light commercial vehicles

Discuss your usual journeys, mileage and load with us. We assess the vehicle and calibration options with everyday efficiency and smooth response in mind. Existing engine or emissions-system faults should be diagnosed first.

Tractors and agricultural machinery

Extensive hands-on tractor and plant experience, including work in manufacturer and main-dealer environments. That practical background informs our approach to agricultural diagnostics, engine management, ECU cloning and calibration. Huey Performance provides an independent service.

Tell us about your tractor, implements, field work and transport duties. Fuel use should be assessed against comparable work completed, load and conditions, rather than an isolated fuel-per-hour reading.

Measure the result

Use consistent before-and-after records: fuel and distance for road vehicles, and fuel per comparable task or area worked for agricultural operations. We do not advertise a blanket percentage saving.

Talk to the specialist

Have the vehicle or machine details, unit part number and fault information ready. We confirm compatibility and the available service before you send anything.
