FAQ

Common questions.

Do you use generic files or outsource programming?

We are professional software programmers with 30 years of experience. All ECU programming and calibration is carried out in-house, working from your original ECU software and tailoring the changes to your vehicle, machine or engine. We do not use bought-in generic tuning files or outsource programming to third-party file providers. You deal directly with the specialist doing the work.

Can you diagnose an ECU without the vehicle?

On-bench diagnostics for compatible ECUs without the vehicle present. We can assess communication, stored fault codes and supported ECU functions on the bench. Some faults require the vehicle, wiring or additional modules for a complete diagnosis.

Can you calibrate or remove fault codes?

Application-specific fault-code calibration, including removal of non-essential codes where technically appropriate and permitted for the intended use. We never disable or remove safety-related fault codes or warnings, or codes required for emissions compliance.

Do you cover the whole UK?

Yes. Huey Performance is being set up around a UK-wide mail-in ECU service. Contact us first so we can confirm the job and what needs to be sent.

Do I need to send the whole vehicle?

For many services, no. The ECU can often be removed and posted to us. Some jobs may still require the vehicle or additional modules, so always contact us first.

Do you work on tractors and diggers?

Yes. Agricultural and plant ECU work is a major part of the service, including tractors, diggers, loaders, telehandlers and other machinery.

Can I send an ECU without calling first?

Please contact us before sending anything. We need the ECU number and vehicle or machine details so we can confirm suitability and avoid unnecessary postage.

Do you offer DPF and AdBlue help?

Yes. We provide diagnostic and specialist support for DPF, SCR and AdBlue faults. Work is assessed according to the application, intended use and relevant legal requirements.
