ECU Cloning & Engine Management

Specialist cloning for engine control units and compatible electronic modules across our vehicle and machinery sectors.

Discuss your job

Cloning is a core service

We assess engine control units (ECUs), engine control modules (ECMs) and compatible engine-management systems for cloning, data transfer and replacement-unit programming. Our coverage spans cars, light commercials, HGVs, tractors, plant, material handling and marine engines.

Cloning transfers the required data from an original unit to a compatible replacement. Suitability depends on the hardware, software, security system and whether the original data can be recovered. Not every unit can be cloned, so contact us before buying a donor ECU.

On-bench diagnostics

On-bench diagnostics for compatible ECUs without the vehicle present. We can assess communication, stored fault codes and supported ECU functions on the bench. Some faults require the vehicle, wiring or additional modules for a complete diagnosis.

Fault-code calibration

Application-specific fault-code calibration, including removal of non-essential codes where technically appropriate and permitted for the intended use. We never disable or remove safety-related fault codes or warnings, or codes required for emissions compliance.

What to send us first

  • Vehicle, machine or engine details and a description of the fault.
  • Clear labels and part numbers from the original and proposed replacement units.
  • Details of water, fire, impact damage or previous programming attempts.

We confirm the available approach and which units need to be sent. Ask about cloning even if your application is not listed.

Talk to the specialist

Have the vehicle or machine details, unit part number and fault information ready. We confirm compatibility and the available service before you send anything.
