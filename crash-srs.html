Support after a collision

We provide electronic diagnostic and module support for crash-damaged vehicles, including engine ECU recovery, compatible replacement-unit programming and assessment of SRS airbag control modules.

SRS reset enquiries

SRS means Supplemental Restraint System. A crash-data reset may be available for a supported, undamaged control module where the vehicle manufacturer's repair procedure permits it. If that procedure requires replacement, the module must be replaced.

A reset does not repair deployed airbags, pretensioners, damaged wiring or other restraint components, and does not certify the vehicle as safe. The complete system needs the prescribed repairs, inspection and diagnostic checks by a qualified repairer before the vehicle returns to use.

Before sending a module

Provide the vehicle details, module part number, diagnostic codes, deployment history and repairs already completed. We assess suitability before agreeing the work. This service concerns vehicle electronics; it is not structural or body repair.

Talk to the specialist

Have the vehicle or machine details, unit part number and fault information ready. We confirm compatibility and the available service before you send anything.
