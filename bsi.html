Body electronics and replacement units

A BSI (Built-in Systems Interface) manages multiple body-electronics functions on vehicles fitted with this type of unit. It is distinct from the engine ECU and the SRS airbag module.

We assess BSI faults, compatible cloning or data transfer, replacement-unit programming and configuration recovery. A reset alone will not resolve every fault; the unit and vehicle symptoms determine the appropriate service.

Check compatibility before buying

Send the original and replacement unit numbers, vehicle details, fault codes and symptoms. We will confirm whether the proposed replacement is suitable and whether access to the vehicle or additional modules is required.

Talk to the specialist

Have the vehicle or machine details, unit part number and fault information ready. We confirm compatibility and the available service before you send anything.
